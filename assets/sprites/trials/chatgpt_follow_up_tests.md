# Follow-up tests

Paste one of these at a time, in the same ChatGPT conversation, after Test 1 has been checked. They rely on the brief already being there.

## Test 2: the far arm, 4 frames

```
Test 2. Same character, same camera, same rules as before.

Standing, feet still, one row of 4 frames. His LEFT arm, the one farther from the viewer,
does all the work. His RIGHT arm, the near one, hangs at his side with a closed fist and
does not move in any frame.

1. Standing as in the first walk frame, both fists at his sides.
2. His left arm lifts: left elbow raised to shoulder height and pointing forward, left fist
   beside his left ear.
3. His left fist reaches back over his left shoulder to the middle of his back, as if
   gripping a shield slung there. His left elbow points up.
4. His left arm swings forward and stops with the forearm level across the front of his
   chest, fist closed, as if holding a shield in front of him.

The left arm passes behind his head and body where it should. Do not swap the arms, and do
not draw anything in his hands.
```

## Test 3: crawl cycle, 6 frames

```
Test 3. Same character, same camera, same rules as before.

A crawl on hands and knees, facing right, one row of 6 frames that loops. His back is level
and his head is up, looking ahead. From the ground to the top of his head he is no more than
half his standing height. His robe drapes over his back and trails between his legs.

1. Right hand and left knee forward, left hand and right knee back.
2. Right hand and left knee planted and taking his weight; left hand lifting.
3. Left hand and right knee passing forward, halfway.
4. Left hand and right knee forward, right hand and left knee back. The mirror of frame 1.
5. Left hand and right knee planted and taking his weight; right hand lifting.
6. Right hand and left knee passing forward, halfway. This leads back into frame 1.

Hands and knees rest on the same invisible ground line in every frame. His body length is
the same in every frame.
```
