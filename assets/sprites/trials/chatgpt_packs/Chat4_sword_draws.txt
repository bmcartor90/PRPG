Sprite sheet for a 2D side-scrolling platformer.

The attached picture is my character, Lucca. The large figure on the left is him standing.
On the right are his walk frames (top row) and run frames (bottom row). Match him exactly:
the same face, hair, robe, colours, outline and pixel style. Do not redesign him.

CAMERA
- Fixed and side-on, level with his waist, no perspective distortion.
- He faces right with his chest turned very slightly toward the viewer, as in the picture.
- That angle never changes in any frame, whatever he is doing. We never see him from the
  front or from behind. Only his limbs and spine move.
- Facing right, his right arm is the near arm and his left arm is the far arm.

RULES
- One landscape image, 1536 x 1024. A truly transparent background.
- Frames in equal-sized cells, in order, left to right, with clear space between them.
  Nothing touching, nothing cut off at an edge.
- The same scale in every frame: his head is the same size in every frame.
- Hands are empty closed fists.
- No text, labels, grid lines, ground lines, shadows, glows or motion lines.
- No scenery or props: no ledge, rope, wall or weapon. Only Lucca.
- The LAST frame is always Lucca standing exactly like the large figure in the picture.
  Draw it fresh; do not paste it in.

draw no sword, no shield, no sheath and no strap. The game adds those itself. Only the arm
named moves; the other arm hangs at his side with a closed fist and does not move.
