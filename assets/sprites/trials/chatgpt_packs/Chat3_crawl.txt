Sprite sheet for a 2D side-scrolling platformer.

The attached picture is my character, Lucca. The large figure on the left is him standing.
On the right are his walk frames (top row) and run frames (bottom row). Match him exactly:
the same face, hair, robe, colours, outline and pixel style. Do not redesign him.

CAMERA
- Fixed and side-on, level with his waist, no perspective distortion.
- He faces right with his chest turned very slightly toward the viewer, as in the picture.
- That angle never changes in any frame, whatever he is doing. We never see him from the
  front or from behind. Only his limbs and spine move.
- Facing right, his right arm is the near arm and his left arm is the far arm.

RULES
- One landscape image, 1536 x 1024. A truly transparent background.
- Frames in equal-sized cells, in order, left to right, with clear space between them.
  Nothing touching, nothing cut off at an edge.
- The same scale in every frame: his head is the same size in every frame.
- Hands are empty closed fists.
- No text, labels, grid lines, ground lines, shadows, glows or motion lines.
- No scenery or props: no ledge, rope, wall or weapon. Only Lucca.
- The LAST frame is always Lucca standing exactly like the large figure in the picture.
  Draw it fresh; do not paste it in.

FRAMES: a low crawl on hands and knees that loops, 7 frames in one row.

He faces right. He is LOW: from the ground to the top of his head he is no more than 40% of
his standing height. His back is level and his head is level with his back, chin pushed
forward, not raised. His shins lie flat on the ground behind his knees. On this sheet only,
a hand that is planted is open and flat on the ground. His robe drapes over his back and
trails between his legs. Hands and knees rest on the same invisible ground line in every
frame, and his body is the same length in every frame.

His arms and legs move in opposite pairs, and the legs must clearly alternate: when one
knee is forward under his hips, the other is well behind.

1. Right hand far forward, left knee forward under his hips. Left hand back by his chest,
   right knee far back.
2. Weight on the right hand and left knee. Left hand lifted off the ground and moving
   forward. Right knee lifted and sliding forward.
3. Left hand and right knee passing the other pair, halfway.
4. Left hand far forward, right knee forward under his hips. Right hand back by his chest,
   left knee far back. The mirror of frame 1.
5. Weight on the left hand and right knee. Right hand lifted and moving forward. Left knee
   lifted and sliding forward.
6. Right hand and left knee passing the other pair, halfway. This leads back into frame 1.
7. Standing, exactly like the large figure in the picture.
